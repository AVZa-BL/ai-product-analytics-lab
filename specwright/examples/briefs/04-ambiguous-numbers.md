# Comet Racers: garage upgrade prompt

Product asked for a quick test of a new "upgrade your car" prompt in the garage.

**Hypothesis.** The new prompt makes more players upgrade a car in their first week after seeing
the garage. The old prompt is the control; the new one is variant_a. Assignment is per player,
50/50, at the moment a player first opens the garage.

**Who.** Existing players who open the garage. A few thousand players open it every day.

**Metric.** Share of players who upgrade a car within 7 days: 22% today. We hope for roughly a
10% relative lift. Alpha 0.05, power 80%, two-sided.

**Schedule.** We would run it for about two weeks and certainly not less than a week.

**Procedure.** We will look at it when it is done. Check the sample ratio daily. Owner: racing-live.
