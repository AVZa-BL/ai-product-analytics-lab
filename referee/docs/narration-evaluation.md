# Narration evaluation: the router on the held-out half

Generated by `python tests/narration_evaluation.py --write` from `tests/data/narration_eval/heldout_first_score.json` and a fresh run of the router (design section 24.10); do not edit. It reports what was built and tested offline: the router of the narration layer (`src/referee/narration/router.py`) on 64 agent-authored questions that were held out of its construction by procedure. No model was run through Referee. The counts are agreement with the agent-authored taxonomy (`tests/data/narration_eval/taxonomy.json`); they say nothing about usefulness, readability, a model's behaviour or real users' phrasing, none of which was measured.

## The checker study

`docs/grounding-study.md` measures what a closed-vocabulary checker accepts over enumerations of short texts: a checker's acceptance space, not what a model can or cannot do. No model was run for it or through Referee. Its figures, as `docs/grounding-study.json` states them: 0 of the 81 catalogue sentences contain none of the study's loaded words under its full list and 69 under the digits-only ablation, and the checker admitted 0 of 28 hand-written harmful probes under the full list (9 and 26 of 28 under the qualifiers-and-digits and digits-only ablations) (docs/grounding-study.md).

## Known gaps, and what is not verified

The known gaps are those of design section 24.13; this report adds none and removes none. Those that bear on the figures above:

- The held-out half is a procedure, not a technical barrier, and its questions, labels and reviews come from one kind of agent. The counts are agreement with an agent-authored taxonomy, not recall on real users' phrasing.
- Three questions per refusal category and five per intent rest on very little; no per-category figure should be read as a rate.
- The router is not injection-proof and its twelve categories are not complete. A question answered when it should have been refused gets the fixed page, which prints every finding.

Not verified: the model path has never been run with a model. Nothing here was run on macOS. That a page is byte-identical between machines is not checked until CI runs it.

## Reproduce

From `referee/`, with `src` and `tests` on `PYTHONPATH`:

- `python tests/narration_evaluation.py` prints the current score beside the first; it refuses until the first score exists.
- `python tests/narration_evaluation.py --first-score` takes the first score, once.
- `python tests/narration_evaluation.py --write` writes these two documents.
