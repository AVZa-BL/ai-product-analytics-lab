## The loaded words

A sentence is free under a view when none of its tokens is loaded under it. A token is a chunk between spaces and hyphens, in lower case, without the punctuation at its ends; one that holds a digit or a symbol (`5%`, the `007` of `RES-007`, `min_duration_days`) is a marker and is loaded under every view. The list holds the words that carry a decision, a condition, a negation, a modal, a quantity, a comparison, a certainty, a cause, a severity or verdict, a unit, a position, an arm or an ordinal, in the folded form of the tokeniser (a trailing plural s off). It is one agent's judgement, made with the sentences in view and read once against those that the qualifiers view left free, after which two of the words added took that count from 11 to 8.
