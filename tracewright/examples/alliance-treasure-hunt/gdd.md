# Alliance Treasure Hunt: design document (synthetic)

*A made-up feature for a made-up strategy game, "Realm of Ember". It exists only to demonstrate
Tracewright; nothing here describes a real product.*

## Summary

Alliance Treasure Hunt is a 72-hour event that unlocks every Monday for alliances of at least
five members. Alliances work together to collect Map Fragments, turn them into Treasure Chests
and reach shared milestones.

## How it works

An alliance leader starts a hunt by paying an entry cost of 500 Ember Coins from the alliance vault.
Only one hunt can run per alliance at a time.

Members earn Map Fragments from battles and daily quests.

Ten Map Fragments can be exchanged for one Treasure Chest.

Treasure Chests come in three tiers: Bronze, Silver and Gold. The tier depends on the alliance's
rank when the chest is opened. A chest contains Ember Coins, Gems, Speed-ups or Hero Shards.

When the alliance reaches 100, 250 and 500 opened chests, every member receives a milestone reward.

Members who leave the alliance within two hours of a milestone forfeit their share of that milestone
reward.

Players can buy Fragment Packs for real money, limited to three per day.

## Screens and notifications

A hunt screen shows the alliance's progress bar and a list of members' contributions. Every member
receives a push notification when a milestone is reached.

## Success criteria

- At least 40% of eligible alliances should start a hunt in the first month.
- The event must not reduce day-7 retention for players who do not take part.
- Fragment Packs must stay below 15% of all fragments earned.

## Open design questions

- Should chest contents be allowed to raise battle power? Undecided.
- Should the contribution list be visible to players outside the alliance? Undecided.
